      Statistical robustness tests

      Seven checks that ask whether the results are real, not luck: is a forecast significantly better than another (Diebold-Mariano), did ridership change at known dates (Chow, Bai-Perron), did the models leave patterns behind (Ljung-Box), are the series stationary (ADF and KPSS), which groups differ (Kruskal-Wallis and Dunn), how precise are the key numbers (bootstrap) and does the forecast hold up on many windows (rolling-origin cross-validation). Run statistical_tests.py to fill this tab.
